Getting to Know Moulded Palm Sugar

Moulded palm sugar comes in a solid form often used in traditional Indonesian recipes. It can be added to warm drinks, sauces, cakes, snacks, and home-cooked dishes where the distinctive sweetness of palm sugar is desired.

Cut or shave it according to the recipe. If needed, dissolve it in warm liquid and add gradually to balance sweetness with the other ingredients.
