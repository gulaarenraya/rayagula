Getting to Know Granulated Palm Sugar

Granulated palm sugar comes in small crystals that are easy to measure and blend into recipes. Its distinctive aroma and caramel-like notes can complement coffee, tea, milk-based drinks, cakes, and desserts.

For beverages, add it gradually while stirring until dissolved, then adjust the amount to suit your taste and the other ingredients. In baking, palm sugar may contribute a different colour and flavour from white sugar; the final result depends on the recipe and ingredients.
