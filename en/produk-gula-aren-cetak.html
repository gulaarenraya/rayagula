Product Guide: Moulded Palm Sugar

Moulded palm sugar comes in a solid form often used in traditional Indonesian recipes. It can be added to warm drinks, sauces, cakes, snacks, and home-cooked dishes.

Cut or shave it according to the recipe. If needed, dissolve it in warm liquid and add gradually to balance sweetness with the other ingredients.
