FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does Raya Gula accept wholesale orders?

Please send an enquiry with your product type, volume, and destination country. Minimum order quantities (MOQs) and capacity will be confirmed based on availability.

Are samples available?

Sample availability, costs, and shipping methods must be confirmed through enquiry form.

Does Raya Gula offer private-label products?

The company reports that private-label options may be available. Design, packaging, MOQ, and volume requirements must be confirmed before any commitment.

What is the product shelf life?

The company reports a 12-month shelf life. Storage conditions and the shelf life for each product type should be confirmed in the applicable written specification.

Is international shipping available?

The company targets both the Indonesian and export markets. Shipping routes, documents, Incoterms, destination-country requirements, and shipment feasibility must be confirmed for each order.

What certification information is available?

The company reports Halal, Organic, and SNI certifications. Certificate numbers, issuing bodies, product scope, and validity should be checked against current documents. Buyers may request copies before ordering.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment terms are set out in the quotation and transaction agreement. Please do not assume a particular payment method is available until confirmed.

How can I request a quotation?

Visit Export & Wholesale and provide your company details, destination country, product, and estimated volume.
