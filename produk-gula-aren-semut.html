Panduan Produk: Gula Aren SemutProduct Guide: Granulated Palm Sugar

Gula aren semut berbentuk butiran yang mudah ditakar untuk berbagai kebutuhan dapur. Aroma khas dan nuansa karamel dapat melengkapi kopi, teh, minuman susu, kue, dan dessert.Granulated palm sugar is easy to measure for a wide range of kitchen uses. Its distinctive aroma and caramel-like notes can complement coffee, tea, milk-based drinks, cakes, and desserts.

Untuk minuman, tambahkan sedikit demi sedikit sambil diaduk hingga larut. Untuk baking, sesuaikan takaran dengan resep karena gula aren dapat memengaruhi warna, aroma, dan rasa akhir.For beverages, add it gradually while stirring until dissolved. For baking, adjust the amount to the recipe, as palm sugar can affect the final colour, aroma, and flavour.
