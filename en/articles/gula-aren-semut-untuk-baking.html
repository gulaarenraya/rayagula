Granulated Palm Sugar for Baking: Ideas for the Kitchen

Exploring local flavour in cookies, cakes, desserts, and homemade creations.

Baking offers plenty of room to experiment with local ingredients. One of them is granulated palm sugar. Its convenient granulated form makes it suitable for a variety of baking recipes and desserts.

Palm sugar cookies

Granulated palm sugar can be used as one of the sweetening ingredients in cookie dough. It can give the finished cookies a different colour and flavour character. Try adding it to a favourite cookie recipe, then adjust the taste based on the result you prefer.

Cakes with a touch of palm sugar

Palm sugar can also be used in several types of cake. Pair it with ingredients such as banana, coconut, chocolate, or spices to create a different flavour profile. These combinations can bring variety to familiar recipes.

For desserts and other sweet dishes

Beyond baking, granulated palm sugar can sweeten a range of desserts. You can experiment with pudding, sweet porridge, pancakes, and other sweet dishes. Adjust its use to suit the character of each recipe.

Tips for using granulated palm sugar

Make sure the sugar has been stored properly before use. Start with the amount specified in your recipe, then make adjustments in your next attempt according to your taste. This gives you room to find the flavour balance that works best for each creation.

With granulated palm sugar, the kitchen becomes a place to explore local flavours through modern creations. Start with a simple recipe and discover the combinations you enjoy most with Raya Gula.
