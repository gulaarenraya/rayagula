Getting to Know Liquid Palm Sugar

Liquid palm sugar offers a convenient format for beverages and culinary creations. Depending on the recipe, it can be mixed into milk coffee, tea, cold drinks, sauces, dressings, and desserts.

For drinks, begin with a small amount and stir until evenly combined. For sauces or desserts, add it gradually to maintain the desired sweetness and consistency. The amount needed can vary with the recipe, temperature, and accompanying ingredients.
